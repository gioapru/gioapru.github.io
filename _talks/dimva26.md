This was my first time attending DIMVA as a conference. And I loved it. Contrarily to "big" conferences which are extremely crowded and in which it is hard to meet new people (there are simply too many attendees), this did not happen for DIMVA: I had the pleasure to talk and get acquainted with many researchers outside my "contact list" and I enjoyed all the talks that were given. Plus, the event being held in a wonderful location (Crete) in the best time of the year (early July) made everything even better!

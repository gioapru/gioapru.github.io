For my third time in Dagstuhl, I took part in a seminar of an adjacent community to the ones I typically do research in: Federated Learning. It was great to meet many new faces, but also to see the viewpoint of researchers in a slightly-different domain on a topic that I have never seriously tackled (despite considering doing so many years before...). I got a lot of inspiration from this seminar, and I thank the organizers for inviting me!

This was the second time I gave a lecture talk to this Summer School. The talk was quite similar in structure to the one I gave the <a class="btn btn-outline-primary my-1 mr-1 btn-sm" href="{{ base_path }}/talks/ssie25" target="_blank" rel="noopener">previous year</a>. However, for this one, I covered more recent subjects that I tackled in my research, such as the attack against phishing website detectors presented at <a class="btn btn-outline-primary my-1 mr-1 btn-sm" href="{{ base_path }}/publications/eurosp26" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IEEE EuroS&P '26</a>., the attacks against LLM-driven Algorithmic Trading Systems presented at <a class="btn btn-outline-primary my-1 mr-1 btn-sm" href="{{ base_path }}/publications/satml26b" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IEEE SaTML '26</a>, and the attacks against LLMs used in scientific peer-review published in <a class="btn btn-outline-primary my-1 mr-1 btn-sm" href="{{ base_path }}/publications/taisap26" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ACM TAISAP</a> earlier this year.
